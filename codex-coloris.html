Dei limiti · Evidence boundary

The limits of the evidence

Color does change how people feel and act, but never on its own. Every effect on these pages is moderated by the interface around it, the task, the category, the culture and the audience.12

Nota · freshness

The foundational studies are older than a year because they set the theory. The accessibility and design-system sources were updated in 2025 and 2026.

40studies reviewed

sourcedA 2024 systematic review found color can influence consumers’ psychological state and behavior, but the effect is moderated by other factors.8

Hue alone is incomplete

sourcedBrand-color experiments link red with excitement and blue with competence, yet saturation and lightness also shift perceived personality. Product–color congruity matters: functional offers fit functional colors, sensory or social offers fit expressive ones.91011

muted→vivid dark light

Red for detail, blue for ideas?

sourced, disputedOne influential study reported that red helped detail-oriented work and blue helped creative work. Later research found task difficulty changed the result, and an adequately powered replication failed to reproduce the priming effect.121314

Red sharpens the eye for detail; blue frees the mind to invent.

CaveTreat cognition-by-color claims as hypotheses, never as frontend rules.

f. 2r

Dei principi · Operating principles

Six principles of the workshop

Each principle pairs a design rule with the evidence behind it and the practical catch. The rules are inferred; the reasons are sourced.

Nota

One action color, repeated only for equivalent actions. Everything else stays quieter.

Sales

inferredGive the primary call to action a unique role color that is uncommon elsewhere on the page.

sourcedA famous red-over-green test produced 21% more clicks. Its own reading: the red control stood out on a green-dominant page, not that red always wins.3

CaveA loud button can raise clicks while lowering qualified leads or completed purchases. Measure the entire funnel.

Positivity

inferredUse bright surfaces, restrained saturation, generous whitespace and warm accents for rewards or highlights.

sourcedMarketing research links higher saturation with excitement, and higher lightness with relaxation and liking.1711

CaveHigh saturation across the whole interface creates noise and weakens the call to action.

Trust

inferredUse blue, teal, charcoal or deep green as the structural brand color, then add a distinct action color.

sourcedBrand experiments link blue with competence; cross-cultural work rated blue labels more secure, trustworthy and dependable than red.918

CaveAn all-blue page can make a blue button disappear, and it may look generic.

Urgency

inferredRestrict red to deadlines, errors, destructive actions, or a sales button that has been tested.

sourcedRed can signal excitement, danger, mistakes or avoidance, depending on context.1129

CaveIf red means both Buy and Delete, the system loses its semantic consistency.

Reusability

inferredSeparate brand, action, surface and state roles, so each theme can replace values without touching component logic.

sourcedMaterial Design groups colors into roles; USWDS defines separate families for info, error, warning, success, emergency and disabled.1920

CaveHex values written straight into components cause drift and expensive redesigns.

Comprehension

inferredKeep the same role-to-color mapping across every page and product.

sourcedW3C cognitive guidance recommends consistent colors and appearance for controls, states and focus indicators.21

CaveCampaign teams often add one-off button colors that break learned behavior.

Della macchina · Palette architecture

The three-layer machine

A reusable palette is a gear train. Raw pigments drive meanings, meanings drive components, and a new theme only swaps the first wheel.

Nota

Themes swap values. Components keep consuming the same role tokens.

  1. I
    Primitive
    Raw ramps such as blue-700, orange-700, slate-950.
  2. II
    Semantic
    Intent tokens such as color-action, color-success, color-error.
  3. III
    Component
    Contextual aliases such as button-primary-bg, quiz-correct-border.

sourcedThis follows mature practice: Material defines primary, secondary, tertiary, error, surface and outline roles; USWDS keeps state colors in explicit semantic families.1920

          Degli usi · Use by interface type

          By the kind of interface

          The same palette behaves differently on a landing page, a checkout, a game or a dashboard. Six workshop notes, one for each.

          Nota

          Enemy red, error red, purchase red: three roles that must never share one color.

          Marketing page

          inferredBrand color for recognition and structure, one separate action color for the conversion path. Keep hero, social proof, offer and pricing backgrounds mostly neutral so the button keeps priority, and repeat the action color only for equivalent actions.

          sourcedColor works as an attention, message and affect cue; higher saturation raises excitement and lighter values raise relaxation and liking. Concentrate saturation instead of spreading it.17

          Button only

          inferredChoose its color in this order:

          1. role clarity
          2. page contrast
          3. label contrast
          4. state differences
          5. brand fit
          6. psychological association

          Primary buttons are usually filled, secondary ones quieter, and destructive actions stay red even when another color would stand out more.

          calculatedWhite on #C2410C 5.18 : 1, white on #075985 7.56 : 1, white on #6D28D9 7.10 : 1, #111827 on #F59E0B 8.26 : 1.623

          Store & checkout

          inferredNeutral product surfaces, one add-to-cart color, a distinct but related checkout action, and written labels for stock, discounts, validation and payment. Discounts may use red or orange; the purchase button should not look like an error unless a test proves it pays.

          sourcedCool store environments have often been preferred to warm ones; warm colors raise arousal, cool colors can relax, and shopping motivation moderates both.17

          Gaming

          inferredMatch saturation to intensity. Casual and puzzle games suit bright surfaces and a stable play color; competitive games can carry darker surfaces and higher-chroma accents. Keep faction colors apart from interface meanings.

          sourcedSimilar functions and states should look the same, above all in fast interfaces where a color is learned once and recognized instantly.21

          Education & quizzes

          inferredA calm structural color for navigation and a distinct progress or action color. Save red for final incorrect or error feedback, add an icon and a text label, and never bathe the assessment in red.

          sourced, mixedRed–blue performance findings vary by task, difficulty and study; exam research found no meaningful red-versus-green effect. Color organizes learning; it is not a cognitive booster.241314

          Business dashboards

          inferredLow-saturation surfaces by default, with chroma only for action, exception and status. In charts and KPIs, pair color with labels, arrows, line styles, shapes or patterns, and keep brand colors apart from risk colors.

          sourcedW3C requires a visible alternative when color carries information, and states and required boundaries need 3 : 1 against neighbors.74

          f. 5r

          Dell’esperienza · Conversion validation

          The experiment

          Every palette is a hypothesis. Leonardo trusted experience over authority; so should the funnel. Run the steps in order.

          Nota

          A click is not the business event. Count the lead, the checkout, the finished lesson.

          1. IDefine the business event: a qualified lead, a completed checkout, a finished lesson or a retained player, not merely a click.
          2. IIAudit the page’s dominant colors and reserve one role for the primary action.
          3. IIIVerify contrast and non-color cues before experimenting.
          4. IVTest one variable at a time. Don’t change color, copy, size and placement together.
          5. VRun through a complete business cycle and to a sample threshold declared in advance.
          6. VIInspect downstream quality, mobile behavior, accessibility failures and segment differences.
          7. VIIRe-test after material changes to layout, brand palette, offer, traffic source or audience.

          In a 2026 dataset of 32 button-color tests, most changed nothing, and the winning direction varied by page.25

          26changed nothing measurable (81%)
          5beat their control (16%)
          1other result

          sourcedCounts are rounded from the reported 81% and 16% of 32 tests. Contrast and context are good priors; actual conversion impact has to be measured locally.

          Del bivio · Decision framework

          The crossroads

          Six questions to ask before choosing a palette. Each one forks; follow the branch that describes the product.

          Nota

          If green is already the brand, the button must be something else.

          Is the product high-trust or high-risk?

          YesinferredStart with blue, teal or slate structure and conservative saturation.

          NoMore expressive brand hues are viable.

          Is the experience recreational or sensory?

          YesinferredRaise saturation and palette breadth, keeping one action role.

          NoKeep surfaces neutral and chroma sparse.

          Is the urgency genuine?

          YesinferredUse a warm accent locally, with explicit deadline or status copy.

          NoDon’t manufacture urgency with persistent red.

          Is green already the brand color?

          YesinferredChoose a blue, purple, orange or other verified contrasting button.

          NoGreen stays free for success states.

          Does color carry status or correctness?

          YessourcedAdd text, an icon or a shape and verify 3 : 1 differentiation.7

          NoDecorative color may stand alone when it carries no information.

          Is the audience international?

          YessourcedValidate associations in each target market; meanings differ across cultures.2618

          NoLocal brand research still beats generic color charts.

          The recommended palette
